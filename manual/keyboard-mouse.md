# Keyboard and mouse

Keys act on the control you’re using. Enter in the transaction list opens
an entry; Enter in its editor saves it. On a Mac keyboard, Return is the
same action as Enter in these instructions.

## Select transactions

Click a row to select it without opening its editor.

- **Shift-click:** select a range in the displayed order.
- **Command-click** on macOS, or **Ctrl-click** on other platforms:
  add or remove an individual row.
- With the transaction list focused, **Up/Down** moves the selection;
  **Home/End** goes to the first or last row. Hold **Shift** to extend
  the selection.
- **Enter:** open the focused transaction.
- **Right-click:** open the actions for the selection. Check the selected
  rows before changing or deleting them.

[More about selection](find-transactions.md#select-without-editing).

## Edit an entry

**Double-click** a transaction to open or close its editor. Selecting
another row leaves that editor where it is.

- **Tab / Shift+Tab:** move forward or backward between editor fields.
- **Enter** in the date, payee, amount or memo: save the complete edit.
- **Escape:** discard changes since the last save. In an unsaved new
  entry, it cancels the entry.
- In a category field, type to find choices. Use **Up/Down**, then
  **Enter** or **Tab** to choose. **Escape** first closes the suggestions.
  **Shift+Tab** abandons an unchosen category search and moves back.
- **Home/End** in a text field: move to the start or end of its text.
  Hold **Shift** to select the text along the way.

For a saved editor change, use **Undo edit**. In a clean editor,
**Command+Z** on macOS or **Ctrl+Z** on other platforms does the same;
while typing, it undoes text instead. Other actions have their own Undo
controls, and later changes can make Undo unavailable.

[Editing and Undo](add-correct-transactions.md#correct-an-entry).

## Choose a date

Click the calendar button beside a date field.

- Click a day to choose it, or **Today** for today’s date.
- Use the month arrows, or click the month or year to choose another.
- In the day grid, **Left/Right** moves one day; **Up/Down** moves a week.
  **Enter** chooses the date.
- In the month or year grid, arrow keys move through the choices.
  **Enter** returns to the day grid.
- **Escape** returns from month/year choices to the day grid. In the
  day grid, Escape closes the calendar without choosing a date.

Choosing a date fills the field. Save the surrounding edit when ready.
[Dates in the transaction editor](add-correct-transactions.md#choose-a-date).

## Find and move around

- In ledger search, **Enter** applies the search. **Escape** clears it
  and shows the account’s transactions again.
- Click a sortable column heading to sort; click it again to reverse
  the order. **Date** returns to date order.
- Click the arrow beside a transfer category to visit its other posting.
- Use **Back** and **Forward** to return to places you’ve visited.
- Double-click a category or Total amount in a report to see the
  transactions behind it.

[Ledger navigation](find-transactions.md) ·

## Read Help

Choose **Help → SpentIt Help** to return to your reading. For instructions
about the current task, choose **Help for this screen** from that menu or
click **Help** on the screen or dialog. **Search help** beside ledger search
opens examples. In a narrow Help window, **Contents** reveals the topic list
and search.

- **Tab / Shift+Tab** moves between controls. Use **Space** to activate a
  focused button, topic or search result.
- Type in **Search Help**, then click a result or Tab to it and press
  **Space**. **Enter** in the search box opens the first result.
- In the article, **Tab / Shift+Tab** selects links and pictures;
  **Enter** opens the selected one. **Up/Down**, **Page Up/Page Down** and
  **Home/End** scroll the article.
- In a narrow window, **Escape** closes Contents and returns to the article.
- Use **Command+F** on macOS or **Ctrl+F** on other platforms to focus
  **Find in this topic**. Enter advances to the next match; **Previous
  match** goes back.
- **Previous topic / Next topic** follows the guide’s reading order.
  **Back / Forward** follows your visits.
- **Larger text / Smaller text** adjusts the article text.
- Click a picture to enlarge it. Use **Fit**, **Actual size**, or the
  zoom buttons; drag to pan a large picture. With the picture view focused,
  arrow keys pan it. **Close** or **Escape** returns to your reading position.

Use **Close Help** to return to your work, including when a Save / Discard /
Stay dialog is waiting behind Help. Escape in that dialog means Stay.
