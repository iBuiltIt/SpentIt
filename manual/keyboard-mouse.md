# Keyboard and mouse

Keys act on the control you’re using. Enter in the transaction list opens
an entry; Enter in its editor saves it. On a Mac keyboard, Return is the
same action as Enter in these instructions.

## Select transactions

Click a row to select it without opening its editor.

- **Shift-click:** select a range in the displayed order.
- **Command-click** on macOS, or **Ctrl-click** on other platforms:
  add or remove an individual row.
- With the transaction list focused, **Up/Down** moves the selection;
  **Home/End** goes to the first or last row. Hold **Shift** to extend
  the selection.
- **Command+A** on macOS, or **Ctrl+A**, selects every transaction in the
  current list, including rows that are off screen. It leaves out Opening
  Balance. **Command+Shift+A**, or **Ctrl+Shift+A**, clears the selection.
  In a text field those shortcuts act on the text.
- **Page Up / Page Down:** move one screen in the account ledger or
  report transaction list. Hold **Shift** to extend the selection.
  A text field keeps these keys while you type.
- **Enter:** open the focused transaction.
- **Right-click:** open the actions for the selection. Check the selected
  rows before changing or deleting them.
- Select the rows you want to delete. Press **Delete** on Windows and Linux
  or **Forward Delete (⌦)** on a Mac. If your Mac keyboard has only a
  **Delete** key, press **Fn+Delete**. SpentIt asks before deleting anything.

[More about selection](find-transactions.md#select-without-editing).

## Edit an entry

**Double-click** a transaction to open or close its editor. Selecting
another row leaves that editor where it is.

- **Tab / Shift+Tab:** move forward or backward between editor fields.
- **Amount:** Tab, Shift+Tab or click into the field to select the
  amount. Type to replace it, or click again to place the cursor.
- **Enter** in the date, payee, amount or memo: save the complete edit.
- **Escape:** discard changes since the last save. In an unsaved new
  entry, it cancels the entry.
- In a category field, **Tab** moves to the memo if you leave the
  category alone. After typing or pressing **Up/Down**, **Tab** chooses
  the highlighted suggestion. With no match it restores the category
  and moves on. **Enter** chooses a suggestion or stays in the field
  if there is none. **Escape** first closes suggestions, and
  **Shift+Tab** moves back without choosing.
- In **Change category…** from the ledger or report transaction menu, type part of a name.
  **Up/Down** moves through the matches. **Enter** or **Tab** chooses
  the highlighted entry.
  Click a choice to use it, or press **Escape** or click outside to close.
- **Home/End** in a text field: move to the start or end of its text.
  Hold **Shift** to select the text along the way.

For a saved change, press **Command+Z** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+Z** on
Windows and Linux, to undo the newest saved action. Import Undo asks for
confirmation; other saved actions undo straight away. To put a change back,
press **Ctrl+Shift+Z** on Windows and Linux or **Command+Shift+Z** on a Mac.
On Windows, **Ctrl+Y** also works. You can also choose **Edit → Redo**.
Import Undo cannot be redone. While you are typing, Undo and Redo act on
the text instead.

[Editing and Undo](add-correct-transactions.md#correct-an-entry).

## Review, merge and link

- In **Review**, Tab to the list of questions. Press **Up** or **Down** to
  move to a question, then press **Enter** to open its first transaction.
- To merge entries in an account, select them and press **Alt+M** on
  Windows and Linux or **Option+M** on a Mac.
- To link a transfer, select the first entry and press **Alt+L** on Windows
  and Linux or **Option+L** on a Mac. Switch to the other account, select
  the other entry and press the same shortcut again.

[Review duplicates and link transfers](review-duplicates-transfers.md).

## Choose a date

Click the calendar button beside a date field.

- Click a day to choose it, or **Today** for today’s date.
- Use the month arrows, or click the month or year to choose another.
- In the day grid, **Left/Right** moves one day; **Up/Down** moves a week.
  **Enter** chooses the date.
- In the month or year grid, arrow keys move through the choices.
  **Enter** returns to the day grid.
- **Escape** returns from month/year choices to the day grid. In the
  day grid, Escape closes the calendar without choosing a date.

Choosing a date fills the field. Save the surrounding edit when ready.
[Dates in the transaction editor](add-correct-transactions.md#choose-a-date).

## Find and move around

- In ledger search, **Enter** applies the search. **Escape** clears it
  and shows the account’s transactions again.
- Click a sortable column heading to sort; click it again to reverse
  the order. **Date** reverses the whole list, including two
  transactions on the same day. Each row keeps the balance after that
  transaction.
- Click the arrow beside a transfer category to visit its other posting.
- Use **Back** and **Forward** to return to places you’ve visited. On
  Windows and Linux, press **Alt+Left Arrow** or **Alt+Right Arrow**.
  On a Mac, press **Command+[** or **Command+]**. **Command+Left Arrow**
  and **Command+Right Arrow** also work. The buttons look faded when
  there’s nowhere to go in that direction or while SpentIt is busy. If
  SpentIt asks about an unfinished edit, choose **Save**, **Discard** or
  **Stay** before you move on.
- To show or hide the left sidebar, press **Ctrl+Shift+B** on Windows
  and Linux or **Command+Shift+B** on a Mac.
- Tab to an account name in the sidebar, then press **Shift+F10** to open
  its menu. If your keyboard has a **Menu** key, that works too. If you
  change your mind while dragging an account, press **Escape** to put it
  back where it was.
- Double-click a category or Total amount in a report to see the
  transactions behind it.

## Read Help

Choose **Help → SpentIt Help** to return to your reading. For help with
what you’re doing, press **F1** on Windows and Linux or **Command+?** on
a Mac. You can also choose **Help for this screen** from the Help menu
or click **Help** on the screen or dialog. **Search help** beside ledger
search opens examples. In a narrow Help window, **Contents** reveals the
topic list and search.

- **Tab / Shift+Tab** moves between controls. Use **Space** to activate a
  focused button, topic or search result.
- Type in **Search Help**, then click a result or Tab to it and press
  **Space**. **Enter** in the search box opens the first result.
- In the article, **Tab / Shift+Tab** selects links and pictures;
  **Enter** opens the selected one. **Up/Down**, **Page Up/Page Down** and
  **Home/End** scroll the article.
- In a narrow window, **Escape** closes Contents and returns to the article.
- Use **Command+F** on macOS or **Ctrl+F** on other platforms to focus
  **Find in this topic**. Enter advances to the next match; **Previous
  match** goes back.
- **Previous topic / Next topic** follows the guide’s reading order.
  **Back / Forward** follows your visits.
- **Larger text / Smaller text** adjusts the article text.
- Click a picture to enlarge it. Use **Fit**, **Actual size**, or the
  zoom buttons; drag to pan a large picture. With the picture view focused,
  arrow keys pan it. **Close** or **Escape** returns to your reading position.

Use **Close Help** to return to your work, including when a Save / Discard /
Stay dialog is waiting behind Help. **Command+W** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+W**
on other computers, closes Help as well. On Windows, **Ctrl+F4** is the
same close shortcut. In a narrow window that shortcut closes Help even
when Contents is open. **Escape** closes Contents first, and closes Help
once Contents is already closed. On an enlarged picture, **Escape** or
**Close** returns you to the article.

Escape in a Save / Discard / Stay dialog means Stay. In the Rules or
Reports leave dialog, Escape means **Cancel**. Closing Help does not
choose a button for you.

If you’re reading the license or notices in **About SpentIt**, press
**Escape** to go back. Press **Escape** from the first page to close the window.
