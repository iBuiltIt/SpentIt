# Words used in SpentIt

Find a term below, then follow its link for the steps and choices.

## Account

A place you keep or owe money, such as Chequing, Credit card or Cash.
Each SpentIt account has its own currency, opening balance and transactions.
[Set up your accounts](accounts.md).

## Balance and opening balance

The **opening balance** is your starting amount, before the transactions
you’re recording. The **account balance** includes that opening and the
saved transactions, even those not yet accepted. A credit-card debt appears
as a negative balance.
[Set the starting balance](accounts.md#set-the-starting-balance).

## Category and Uncategorized

A category says what money was for, such as Groceries or Income. It can
apply to a whole transaction or one part of a split. **Uncategorized**
means that part has no assigned category yet.

**Food** and **Food: Groceries** are separate categories; the colon helps
organize names. An account name in the category field means a transfer
destination instead.
[Organize spending with categories](categories.md).

## File and backup

Your SpentIt **file** holds your accounts and transactions together.
A **backup** is a separate copy of the file that you make after a
successful close while SpentIt is not running.
**QIF Export** copies transactions as plain text. **SpentIt Export** copies
saved rules, reports and categories, not the transactions.
[Keep a copy of the file](backup-recovery.md#keep-a-copy).

## Import and imported original

An **import** brings transactions into SpentIt from a bank export.
The **imported original** is the transaction as it arrived, available for
comparison after you correct it. A manually entered transaction starts
without one; Merge can attach an imported original later.
[Compare with the imported transaction](add-correct-transactions.md#compare-with-the-imported-transaction).

## Ledger

The account’s transaction list, showing dates, payees, categories, amounts
and running balances. Filtering the list hides nonmatching entries;
it does not remove them or change the account balance.
[Read a transaction](find-transactions.md#read-a-transaction).

## Merge and Link

**Merge** keeps one transaction when entries in the same account describe
the same event. Two purchases with the same date and amount can still be
separate purchases. A date one day off can be the same purchase or a
different one. Compare the transactions in Review before you merge.

**Link** connects the two postings of a transfer between accounts.
Both accounts keep their posting; they are not merged into one entry.
[Review duplicates and link transfers](review-duplicates-transfers.md).

## New and accepted

A **new** transaction has not yet been accepted. Once saved, it stays in
your file and counts in balances and reports.

An **accepted** transaction is one you have marked as checked with
**Accept transactions**. You can still correct it. Accepting covers all
accounts and differs from saving an edit; an unsaved edit still needs
Save or Enter.
[Accept transactions — now or later](accept-transactions.md).

## Payee and memo

The **payee** names who you paid or received money from. A **memo** adds
a description or note. A split can have its own memo too.
[Correct an entry](add-correct-transactions.md#correct-an-entry).

## Refund and net spending

A **refund** returns money from an earlier purchase. Give it the purchase’s
category to see their net cost together.

**Net spending** is what remains after money coming back is taken into
account. The 42.18 Market purchase and 6.00 refund give 36.18 of spending.
Reports show purchases as positive and refunds or income as negative;
including income can make the report total negative.
[Read a report’s answer](reports.md#read-the-answer).

## Report

A view of the relevant parts of transactions for selected accounts,
categories and dates. Reports include saved new and accepted entries,
leave out opening balances and transfer amounts, and keep currencies
separate. A fee in an ordinary category can still count.
[Answer questions with reports](reports.md).

## Rule

A saved instruction for repeated categorization. It matches transaction
or split text and assigns an explicit category to eligible Uncategorized
parts. It may also change a payee or memo; it cannot create a transfer.
[Use rules](rules.md#make-a-market-rule).

## Split

One part of a transaction, with its own amount and category. For example,
a -20.00 Corner shop purchase can have -12.00 Groceries and -8.00 Household.
The parts must add up exactly to the transaction total.
[Divide a purchase into splits](add-correct-transactions.md#divide-a-purchase-into-splits).

## Transaction

An entry recording money coming into or going out of an account, whether
imported or entered by hand. It can contain several splits.
[Add a transaction](add-correct-transactions.md#add-a-missing-purchase).

## Transfer and posting

A **transfer** moves money between your accounts, such as paying your
credit card from Chequing. A **posting** is one account’s side of that
movement: -36.18 in Chequing and +36.18 in Credit card.

Recording the payment as a transfer keeps it out of report spending,
so it does not count the groceries again.
[Review a payment between accounts](review-duplicates-transfers.md#is-it-a-payment-between-accounts).
